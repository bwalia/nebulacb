# Procurement and Purchase Order Policy

## 1. No PO, no pay

Northgate operates a strict "no purchase order, no payment" principle. A valid purchase order
must exist and be approved before a supplier commits work. Invoices received without a
matching purchase order are placed on hold by Accounts Payable and returned to the requesting
budget holder.

## 2. Delegated authority

Purchase orders are approved against the following delegated authority limits:

- Team leader: up to GBP 5,000 per order
- Department head: up to GBP 25,000 per order
- Divisional director: up to GBP 100,000 per order
- Chief Financial Officer: up to GBP 500,000 per order
- Board approval: any commitment above GBP 500,000, and any multi-year commitment with a
  total contract value above GBP 250,000

Splitting a requirement into multiple smaller orders to stay within a lower authority limit is
a disciplinary matter.

## 3. Competitive tendering

Purchases above GBP 25,000 require at least three written quotations. Purchases above
GBP 100,000 require a formal tender run by Group Procurement. A single-source award above
GBP 25,000 requires a written single-source justification approved by the Divisional Director
and retained for seven years.

## 4. Supplier onboarding

New suppliers must complete onboarding before a purchase order is raised. Onboarding includes
company registration verification, bank account verification by independent callback to a
number obtained from public record, sanctions screening, and for suppliers above GBP 100,000
of annual spend, a financial stability check.

Changes to an existing supplier's bank details are treated as high risk and always require an
independent callback verification plus dual authorisation by two Procurement staff. Email
instructions alone are never sufficient.

## 5. Three-way match

Accounts Payable performs a three-way match between the purchase order, the goods receipt
note and the supplier invoice. Tolerances are 5 percent or GBP 100, whichever is lower, on
price and 2 percent on quantity. Variances outside tolerance are routed to the budget holder
for exception approval.

## 6. Payment terms

Standard payment terms are 45 days from invoice date. Small suppliers with annual turnover
below GBP 5 million are paid on 30 day terms under the Group's prompt payment commitment.
