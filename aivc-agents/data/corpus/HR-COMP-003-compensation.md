# Annual Compensation Review Guidelines

## 1. Confidentiality

This document is Restricted. It is available to HR Business Partners and the Executive
Committee only. It must not be shared with line managers outside the review window or with any
employee.

## 2. Review cycle

The annual review cycle opens on 1 March and closes on 15 April, with changes effective from
1 July. Off-cycle adjustments require Group HR Director approval and are limited to
promotions, role changes and documented retention risks.

## 3. Budget and distribution

The Group merit budget for the 2026 cycle is 3.4 percent of eligible base pay. Divisional
budgets may vary by up to 0.5 percentage points either side of the Group figure with
Divisional Director and Group HR Director agreement.

Distribution guidance for merit increases is: no more than 15 percent of a population at the
highest performance rating, no more than 35 percent at the second rating, and no automatic
increase for employees rated below expectations.

## 4. Promotion increases

Promotion increases are targeted at 8 to 12 percent of base pay, and must place the employee
no lower than the minimum of the new grade range. Promotion increases are funded separately
from the merit budget.
