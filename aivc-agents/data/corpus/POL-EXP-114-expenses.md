# Employee Expenses and Travel Policy

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to all permanent employees, fixed-term employees and contractors engaged
by Northgate Industrial Group and its subsidiaries. It replaces POL-EXP-102 with effect from
1 February 2026.

## 2. Approval thresholds

Expense claims up to GBP 250 per claim are approved automatically by the line manager in
Concur. Claims between GBP 250 and GBP 2,000 require line manager approval plus a Finance
Business Partner review. Any single claim above GBP 2,000 requires Finance Director approval
before reimbursement is released.

Claims must be submitted within 60 calendar days of the expense being incurred. Claims
submitted after 90 days will be rejected and require a written exception from the Finance
Director.

## 3. Travel

Standard class rail travel is the default for all domestic journeys. First class rail may be
booked only where the ticket price is lower than the equivalent standard class fare, or where
a journey exceeds four hours and is followed by a same-day client meeting.

Economy class is the default for all flights under six hours. Premium economy is permitted
for flights over six hours. Business class requires Executive Committee approval and is not
available for domestic or intra-European travel.

Hotel spend is capped at GBP 180 per night in London, GBP 140 per night elsewhere in the UK,
and EUR 170 per night in continental Europe. Where the cap cannot be met, employees should
book the lowest available rate and note the reason on the claim.

## 4. Meals and subsistence

The daily subsistence allowance is GBP 40 for UK travel and GBP 55 for international travel
where meals are not otherwise provided. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client
entertainment events, which require prior approval from a director.

## 5. Mileage

Private vehicle mileage is reimbursed at 45 pence per mile for the first 10,000 business
miles in a tax year and 25 pence per mile thereafter, in line with HMRC approved rates.
Electric vehicle charging on business journeys is reimbursed at cost with a receipt.

## 6. Non-reimbursable items

Parking fines, speeding penalties, personal entertainment, gym memberships, and travel
between an employee's home and their contractual base location are not reimbursable under
any circumstances.
