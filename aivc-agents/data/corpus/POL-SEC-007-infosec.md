# Information Security and Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Data classification

All information is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer
personal data, supplier bank details, employee records and unpublished financial results are
classified as Restricted by default. Restricted data may not be copied to personal devices,
personal cloud storage, or removable media.

## 2. Use of generative AI tools

Employees may use the Group's approved AI assistant for Internal and Confidential
information. Restricted data must not be entered into any AI tool, approved or otherwise,
unless the specific use case has been assessed and recorded in the AI use case register held
by Group IT Security.

Any AI system that acts on Group data must log every retrieval and every action it takes, and
must enforce the same access controls as the underlying source system. Output from an AI
system is not an authority for a payment, a contract commitment or a disclosure to a
regulator without human review.

## 3. Access control

Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed quarterly. Privileged access
requires a separate named administrative account and hardware multi-factor authentication.
Shared accounts are prohibited. Service accounts must have a named human owner and credentials
rotated at least every 90 days.

## 4. Incident reporting

Suspected security incidents must be reported to the IT Service Desk within one hour of
discovery. Suspected personal data breaches must additionally be reported to the Data
Protection Officer immediately, because the Group has 72 hours from becoming aware of a
breach to notify the Information Commissioner's Office where the breach is notifiable.

## 5. Passwords and authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all remote access and all cloud applications.
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Password reuse across Group and personal accounts
is prohibited.

## 6. Third party access

Third parties are granted access only through the Group's managed access gateway, time-boxed
to the engagement, and never through shared credentials. All third party access is logged and
reviewed monthly by Group IT Security.
