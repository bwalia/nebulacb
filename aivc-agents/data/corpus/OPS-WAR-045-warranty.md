# Warranty, Returns and Field Failure Handling

## 1. Standard warranty terms

Northgate provides a 24 month parts and labour warranty on all industrial pump assemblies
from the date of commissioning, or 30 months from date of despatch, whichever occurs first.
Consumable items including seals, filters and lubricants are warranted for 6 months.

Extended warranty to 60 months is available on the NG-7000 series where the unit is
commissioned by a Northgate approved installer and serviced at the scheduled 12 month
intervals. Missing a scheduled service voids the extended warranty but not the standard
24 month warranty.

## 2. Return authorisation

No goods may be returned without a Return Material Authorisation number issued by the
Aftersales team. Returns received without an RMA number are quarantined and the customer is
contacted; unclaimed goods are disposed of after 90 days.

## 3. Field failure triage

Reported field failures are triaged into four severity bands:

- S1: safety risk or complete production stoppage. Response within 4 hours, engineer on site
  within 24 hours.
- S2: partial loss of function with production impact. Response within 8 working hours,
  engineer on site within 3 working days.
- S3: degraded performance, no immediate production impact. Response within 2 working days.
- S4: cosmetic or documentation issue. Response within 5 working days.

## 4. Root cause and recurrence

Any S1 failure, and any failure type recurring three or more times on the same product line
within a rolling 12 months, triggers a formal 8D root cause investigation led by Quality
Engineering. The 8D must reach containment within 48 hours of being raised.

## 5. Goodwill

Aftersales managers may authorise goodwill repairs outside warranty up to GBP 2,500 per
incident. Amounts above that require Divisional Director approval and are recorded in the
monthly warranty provision review.
